How a coin plays

1

Launch

Pick a market, a side and a take-profit tier. Creator fees lock to the treasury on-chain.

2

Fees stack

Every trade of your coin pays creator fees. They collect until they reach $50.

3

Position opens

The fees become margin on Hyperliquid, in the market and side you chose.

4

Win or bust

Hit take profit and 80% of the profit buys back and burns your coin. Bust and the coin starts stacking again.
