HYPERPUMP Docs

Launch a coin on pump.fun. Its creator fees become a leveraged long or short on Hyperliquid. This page explains every part.

What it is

HYPERPUMP is a launchpad on Solana. Each coin you launch is a normal pump.fun coin, with one difference: all of its creator fees are locked to the HYPERPUMP treasury. Those fees are the coin's bankroll.

When a coin's fees reach $50, HYPERPUMP is meant to use them as margin to open a leveraged position on Hyperliquid, in the market and on the side the coin's creator picked at launch. A winning position burns the coin and funds a HYPERPUMP buyback. A bust sends the coin back to stacking fees.

How a coin plays

  1. Launch. Connect a Solana wallet, name the coin, add a picture and choose a market, a side and a take-profit tier. One transaction creates the coin and locks its creator fees to the treasury.
  2. Fees stack. Every trade of the coin on pump.fun pays creator fees. They collect in the coin's fee vault until they reach $50.
  3. Position opens. The fees become margin on Hyperliquid. The position uses the market's maximum leverage, so $50 of fees opens a position worth $50 times that leverage.
  4. Win or bust. If the price moves far enough your way, the position takes profit. If it moves against you far enough, it is liquidated.

The coin page at /coin/<address> shows each coin's progress and position details.

Market and side

You can pair a coin with any perpetual market on Hyperliquid: BTC, ETH, SOL, HYPE, memecoins, and the stocks and other markets that third parties list there. Use the search box in the launch form to find one. Prices, available markets and leverage come live from Hyperliquid.

Long wins when the price goes up. Short wins when it goes down.

The leverage is the market's maximum, recorded when your coin launches. Different markets allow different maximums, from a few times up to 40x on BTC. The market, side and tier are locked for the life of the coin.

Take-profit tiers

The tier is how much the position has to grow before it takes profit and closes. It is measured against the margin, the $50 of fees that opened it.

Chill2x marginabout 1 in 2$50

Hyper-Degen10x marginabout 1 in 10$450

The price move a tier needs depends on the leverage: (tier − 1) ÷ leverage. The position busts when the price moves 1 ÷ leverage against it. So a higher tier is a bigger prize with lower odds, and the odds are about 1 in the tier on any market.

On a low-leverage market a high tier can need a huge move. HYPERPUMP switches off any tier that would need more than a 100% price move, because the position could sit for months. The launch form shows the move each tier needs for the market you chose.

Fees and the treasury

pump.fun lets a coin's creator share its fees with other addresses. HYPERPUMP's launch transaction sets that up so 100% of the coin's creator fees go to the treasury and the admin rights are revoked, so the split cannot be changed afterward.

The server checks this on-chain before it lists a coin. A coin whose fees are not locked to the treasury is not listed.

Fee progress is read from the coin's fee vault and converted to dollars with the current SOL price. The $50 threshold is the same for every coin.

Creator wallets. pump.fun may pool a creator wallet's fees across all its coins, so a wallet that launches several coins could see the same fee number on each. Using a separate wallet per coin avoids that.

Win or bust

Win

The position takes profit at its tier. The profit is split: 80% buys back and burns the coin, which removes supply, and 20% funds a buyback of HYPERPUMP.

Bust

If the price moves against the position by 1 ÷ leverage, Hyperliquid liquidates it and the margin is lost. The coin is not burned and starts stacking fees again toward its next $50.

Risks

  • Leveraged trading is risky. Positions can be liquidated quickly and the margin can be lost entirely.
  • Most coins on pump.fun lose value. A burn only helps if the coin has buyers.
  • Leveraged products are restricted in some places, including the U.S. and Ontario. Check the rules where you live.
  • Odds are estimates that ignore fees, funding and slippage.
  • This is early software. Do not put in more than you can lose. Nothing here is financial advice.

FAQ

Can I get my fees back?

No. Once a coin is launched its fees are locked to the treasury and cannot be redirected.

Can I change the market, side or tier later?

No. They are set at launch and apply for the life of the coin.

Which wallets work?

Phantom, Solflare and Backpack.

What does launching cost?

The normal pump.fun launch cost and network fees. You can also make an optional first buy of your coin in the same flow.

Where do I follow updates?

@HyperPumpSol on X.
